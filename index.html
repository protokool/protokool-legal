Privacy Policy for ProtoKool

Effective Date: November 2026
Publisher: ProtoKool
Contact: protokool.app@gmail.com

1. Overview & Local-First Storage

ProtoKool is designed as an offline-first property inspection and handover utility. All property records, room checklists, utility meter readings, signatures, and inspection photos are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a backend server that collects or stores your inspection data.

2. User-Initiated Sharing & Exports

You may choose to export or share .protokool packages, A4 PDF reports, or ZIP archives using your device's standard share and file management features. These files are transmitted only when and to whom you explicitly choose.

3. Device Permissions

  • Camera & Photo Library: Used solely to capture or attach photos of property rooms, flaws, keys, and utility meters.
  • Storage / Files: Used to save and import backups, PDFs, and protocol files on your device.

4. In-App Purchases

Optional one-time upgrades (Standard and Pro) are processed securely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your credit card or payment details; Google Play provides only purchase entitlement status so the app can unlock purchased features.

5. Data Deletion

Because your inspection records reside locally on your device, you can delete individual properties directly within ProtoKool at any time, or uninstall the app to remove all locally stored application data.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at protokool.app@gmail.com.
